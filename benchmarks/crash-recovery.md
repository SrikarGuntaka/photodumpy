## Why the reclaim takes as long as it does

A SIGKILLed worker releases nothing, so its work waits on four things in sequence:

| Step | Adds |
|---|---|
| Lease expires: 60 s after its last renewal, which was 0–5 s before the kill | 55–60 s |
| Next reaper pass reclaims it (every surviving worker reaps every 15 s) | 0–15 s |
| First-retry backoff before the job is claimable (2 s ±25%) | 1.5–2.5 s |
| A worker frees a slot and claims it | about 0–2 s here |

That is a window of roughly **57–80 s**. The delay is deliberate: a shorter lease recovers faster but risks reclaiming work from a worker that is merely slow, running it twice. A clean `docker compose stop` releases leases immediately instead of waiting.
